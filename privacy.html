What is public. The BIES app is built on Nostr, an open network. Your public profile, approved directory listings and anything you post publicly are signed with your Nostr key and copied to Nostr servers ("relays"): ours, and public relays run by others (relay.damus.io, relay.primal.net, relay.nostr.band, nos.lol and purplepag.es). Anyone can see them, in any Nostr app. When you delete something, or your account, we delete it from our systems and our relay and ask the other relays to delete it too, but we cannot force them to.

Your Nostr key. If you sign up with email, we create your Nostr key and keep its private part encrypted on our server, so you don't have to manage it. This means we could technically act as you on Nostr; we only use it to sign what you do in the app. You can take your key at any time (Settings → Take your key), and we then delete our copy. If you sign in with your own Nostr key, we never see your private key.

6. Cookies

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Our visit counter works without cookies.

The ticket shop (tickets.buildinelsalvador.com) uses only the cookies it needs to keep your basket, your language choice and your checkout secure. We use no advertising or tracking cookies anywhere. The three cookies are:

  • __Host-pretix_session keeps your basket and checkout. It lasts up to 2 weeks.
  • __Host-pretix_csrftoken protects your checkout from forged requests. It lasts up to 1 year.
  • pretix_language remembers the language you chose. It lasts up to 10 years.

You can delete them in your browser at any time. The shop will simply forget your basket and language.

The BIES app sets no cookies. It keeps you signed in by storing a session token on your device (in your phone's secure storage, or in your browser on app.buildinelsalvador.com), and remembers settings such as your language. It uses no advertising or tracking.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the data we hold about you (access)
  • correct it (rectification)
  • delete it (cancellation, and the right to be forgotten)
  • stop using it for a purpose, including marketing (opposition)
  • give you a copy in a common format to take elsewhere (portability)
  • limit how we use it while a question is resolved (limitation)

Email [privacy@buildinelsalvador.com] with your name, what you are asking for, and how to reach you. We also accept the Agency's official forms (ace.gob.sv/page/formularios). We answer within 20 business days. If we need longer for a good reason, we will tell you, and we can take at most 20 more. If something is missing from your request, we will ask you once.

In the BIES app you can edit your profile at any time, take your Nostr key (Settings → Take your key), and delete your account (Settings → Delete account). You can also ask us to delete it without the app: see Delete your BIES account.

To withdraw consent, use the unsubscribe link in any of our emails, or email us. It is free, it takes effect within 5 business days, and it does not undo what was done before.

If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, you can complain to the Agencia de Ciberseguridad del Estado (ace.gob.sv).

8. How we protect it

All our sites use encrypted connections. Only the team members who need your data can see it, and our shared passwords are kept in a password manager. Nostr keys we hold for you are stored encrypted. We back up the BIES app database every day and keep backups for up to 90 days; any copy kept outside our server is encrypted.
