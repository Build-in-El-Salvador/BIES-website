Membership questions

For help choosing a plan, email the BIES team.

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes. Join Community to get to know BIES on Skool. You can apply for Builder or Pioneer whenever you’re ready.

When do I book my onboarding call?

Builder and Pioneer members book a 15-minute call after payment is confirmed. Use the same email address you used on Satlantis. Free Community members don’t need a call.

How do Pioneer calls work?

Each Pioneer member gets a private, 60-minute session with the BIES advisory team each month. Annual membership includes 12 sessions, one per month.

How does billing work?

Builder is $150 every 3 months or $500 per year. Pioneer is $300 every 3 months or $1,000 per year. Annual billing saves $100 for Builder or $200 for Pioneer compared with four quarterly payments. Choose quarterly or annual billing when you apply on Satlantis.

Where can I meet the community in person?

We host networking gatherings, roundtables, workshops, and other meetups. See the BIES events calendar for upcoming events and registration details.
