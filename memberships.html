Membership questions

For help choosing a plan, email the BIES team.

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes. Join Community to get to know BIES on Skool. You can apply for Builder or Pioneer whenever you’re ready.

Who needs a membership interview?

Builder and Pioneer applicants book a 15-minute conversation with the BIES team. We’ll talk about what you’re building and what you hope to get from the community. Free Community membership requires no interview.

How do Pioneer calls work?

Each Pioneer member gets a private, 60-minute session with the BIES advisory team each month. Annual membership includes 12 sessions, one per month.

How does annual billing work?

Builder is $500 per year and Pioneer is $1,000 per year. Each annual price is billed as a single payment and is equal to 10 months at the monthly price. You’ll choose your plan and billing period on Satlantis after booking your interview.

Where can I meet the community in person?

We host networking gatherings, roundtables, workshops, and other meetups. See the BIES events calendar for upcoming events and registration details.

Book your membership interview

The interview is a 15-minute call with Michael or a BIES team member. Use the same email to book and apply on Satlantis so we can match your booking to your application.

Can’t find a time that works? Email BIES to schedule.

Submit your application using the email you booked with. The BIES team will review your application after the interview.
