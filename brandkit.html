Brand Kit

For partners, sponsors and members of Build in El Salvador.

These are our official logos, plus the full brand guidelines. Use them to show you work with Build in El Salvador — on your site, your deck, your booth, your socials. One download, everything you need to place the mark on any background.

Please don’t pass this page on. We share it directly with the people we work with, so we know where our logo is being used. If someone needs it, send them to info@buildinelsalvador.com and we’ll get them set up.

Download the brand kit (ZIP, 4.7 MB)

What’s inside

Each mark comes three ways: light background for white and pale colors, dark background for navy and dark colors, and white for photos and busy backgrounds. All are PNG with transparency, around 1080px on the long side.

The kit also holds our brand guidelines — 23 pages on clear space, minimum sizes, which mark belongs where, the full color scales and our typography. Worth a look before you put the logo somewhere permanent.

Our colors

  • Deep Navy #121E5A
  • Cobalt Blue #0047AB
  • Blaze Orange #FF5B00
  • Snow #F9F5F4

Using the logo

Please do

  • Use it to show you’re a Build in El Salvador partner, sponsor or member.
  • Follow the clear space and minimum sizes set out in the guidelines.
  • Pick the version that contrasts with your background.

Please don’t

  • Stretch, rotate, recolor or add effects to it.
  • Rebuild the lockup, change the typeface, or crop the mark.
  • Place it on a background that makes it hard to read.
  • Use it in a way that suggests we endorse a product or company we haven’t.

The logo and the Build in El Salvador name are ours. We’re glad to see them used by the people we actually work with, and we will ask anyone else to take them down.

Need something else?

Vectors for print or large-format signage, a one-off format, or a co-branded lockup — just ask. Write to info@buildinelsalvador.com.

Our display typeface, PP Formula Narrow, is licensed to us and isn’t ours to pass on, so it isn’t in the kit.
