Our Story

Build in El Salvador began in 2025, during a period of transition.

Michael Ruiz and Luis Esparragoza had recently moved on from a project and were considering what to build next. Rather than remain idle, they created a Substack publication called Building El Salvador.

The original idea was simple: document the stories of the people helping shape the country's future.

They began interviewing entrepreneurs who had taken the leap of moving to a new country, as well as Salvadorans who were already building businesses, communities, and new opportunities on the ground. Interview after interview, they encountered people with remarkable stories, ambitious ideas, and a genuine belief in El Salvador's potential.

Through those conversations, they began to realize just how much was happening across the country, and how little of it was being connected or documented.

The next stage of BIES emerged unexpectedly.

During an informal gathering at Club Cocal, Michael felt a unique energy in the room. The gathering brought together friends who also happened to be entrepreneurs, builders, and ambitious thinkers. El Salvador naturally moves through periods when momentum feels high and others when it feels quieter, but that evening revealed what could happen when the right people came together.

Michael brought the idea to Luis: what if they could intentionally recreate that energy?

After months of interviewing and writing about entrepreneurs across El Salvador, they began thinking about how to bring those people into the same room. That led to the first BIES roundtable.

The gathering confirmed what Michael had experienced at Club Cocal. When people with bold ideas, complementary skills, and a willingness to help one another come together, meaningful connections form. Conversations lead to collaborations, introductions create opportunities, and ideas begin moving forward.

The publication was no longer only documenting the ecosystem. It was bringing the ecosystem together.

Events became the second part of BIES, but it quickly became clear that occasional gatherings were not enough. The conversations needed somewhere to continue. Members needed a way to follow up, exchange information, make introductions, and stay connected between events.

From that need, the digital community was born.

What began as a simple publication gradually developed into a connected ecosystem of media, events, and community.

Today, BIES is a growing network of entrepreneurs, builders, creators, investors, and local partners who see El Salvador as a place where meaningful ideas can take root. Some were born here, while others chose to make the country their home. What connects them is a shared desire to build, contribute, and turn their ideas into something real.

BIES was not created from a detailed master plan. It grew one story, one gathering, and one relationship at a time.

It continues to grow in the same way: through the people who believe in El Salvador's potential and are willing to help build what comes next.

Our Core Values

How We Operate

The quality of our network is preserved through a set of values that define our culture and how we operate. It ensures discussions stay productive, collaborations remain progress-oriented, and our network continues to be a positive force for its members and stakeholders.

1

We empower individual agency.

We are high-agency builders who take initiative in creating the world we want to see. We don't default to powerlessness or blame. We see an opportunity and take bold action, we move forward even in the face of imperfect conditions, and we respond to challenges with resilience, creativity, and optimism.

2

We collaborate towards a more prosperous future.

We believe that collaboration, not competition, is the path to prosperity. We operate on a low time preference, focused on building long-term relationships and creating long-term value. We speak up when we see other members engaging in short-term, transactional, or extractive behaviors.

3

We build on transparent, open, honest foundations.

We seek to build on a foundation that utilizes open and decentralized protocols, where trust is verifiable through proof of work, and where sound money is transacted to secure the value we create. We recognize the pitfalls of a closed and centralized system. And we believe this foundational layer should be Bitcoin-native.

4

We leverage technology that liberates and uplifts our humanity.

We embrace technology that elevates the human spirit. We don't shun technological progress. Rather, we leverage emerging technologies such as AI to unlock new levels of human agency, capability, and coordination. We understand that abundance is downstream of technological innovation.

5

We embrace a regenerative way of living.

We acknowledge that many of our current systems are unsustainable. These systems extract value in preference for the here and now, at the cost of society and human flourishing down the line. Rather than participating in these systems, we seek out or build alternative structures that sustain humanity, abundance, and harmony with nature.

How We Contribute to El Salvador's Growth & Revitalization

The National Bitcoin Office (ONBTC) under President Nayib Bukele envisions dynamic new projects emerging from El Salvador that contribute to the nation's revitalization and rebirth. A Renaissance 2.0.

Our network helps advance this vision in two important ways. First, we attract entrepreneurs, investors, and other bright minds to El Salvador, who see this new renaissance as a window of opportunity. And not only that, we keep talent in the country with a supporting ecosystem. Second, we provide education and employment opportunities to Salvadorans who want to see their country grow, succeed, and prosper through generations.

Our network contributes to the growth and development of El Salvador through:

  • Innovation
  • Highly-skilled talent
  • Job creation
  • Outside investment
  • A Bitcoin-native economy
  • Educational workshops and training sessions
  • Tourism
  • Circular economies
